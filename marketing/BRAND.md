# Skivvies — Brand

**Name:** Skivvies.org (full name: Skivvies Project)
**Domain:** skivvies.org
**Handles:** @skivvies.org on Instagram, facebook.com/skivvies.org
**Parent:** a program of Cornerstones Recovery Inc, a 501(c)(3)
**Who we serve:** men and women entering addiction treatment in Stark County, Ohio — starting at a men's residential program in Massillon (don't name it publicly until CommQuest says OK)

## Brand line
**Because Rock Bottom Shouldn't Be Bare Bottom.**

## Tagline
New basics for people starting recovery.

## Colors and type
- Coral #f35d40 · Ink #102520 · Cream #fff6f0
- Big, heavy, tight headlines; plain friendly body text
- Simple line icons (underwear, sock, heart, church, box). No photos of people in treatment.

## Rules
- 3-second test: if they don't get it in under 3 seconds, redo it.
- Church-safe: funny is good, crude is out. Churches run Undie Sunday.
- NEW underwear only. Say it every time.
- Respectful words: "people starting recovery," never "addicts."
- American spelling. No "should" in our copy.
- No dollar asks until online giving is live (Cornerstones bank account + Stripe).
